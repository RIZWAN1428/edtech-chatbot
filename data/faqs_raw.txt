--- Onboarding / Getting Started ---

hey so a lot of users ask how do I create an account on EduSpark?? 
answer we usually give: Go to eduspark.com, click Sign Up, enter email + password OR
use Google/Facebook login, verify email via link sent, done. takes 2 mins.

Q - how to reset password
forgot password link on login page, enter registered email, reset link sent, expires in 30 min

users often confused about "how do i pick a course" -- 
tell them: browse Catalog, use filters (subject, level, price), click course card to see
syllabus + reviews, hit Enroll.

Free trial? -- yes we give 7 day free trial on all Pro courses, no card needed for basic trial,
card needed only if they want premium trial (rare case)

--- Order / Enrollment Tracking ---

"where is my course" type queries = basically order tracking equivalent for us since its digital
answer: once payment done, course appears INSTANTLY under My Learning tab. if not showing,
tell them to refresh / re-login. if still missing check payment status first.

payment pending / stuck - happens sometimes with UPI, usually resolves in 15 min, if not
resolved in 2 hrs then escalate to billing team

certificate delivery Q - certificates auto generate after course completion (100% modules +
passing quiz score 60%+), downloadable from Certificates section, usually instant but can
take up to 24hrs during high traffic

can I track my learning progress - yes, Dashboard shows % complete per course, streak counter,
time spent

--- Refund / Cancellation ---

refund policy!! important one, get asked a LOT
- full refund within 7 days of purchase IF less than 20% course content consumed
- after 7 days = no refund, but can request course SWAP (credit transfer to another course)
  within 30 days
- subscription plans (monthly/annual) - cancel anytime, no refund for current billing period,
  access continues till period end
- refund processing time 5-7 business days back to original payment method

how to cancel subscription - Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan, confirm, done. no phone
call needed unlike some competitors lol

annual plan cancelled mid year - no partial refund, standard policy, sometimes agents give
goodwill exceptions but that's case by case NOT a guarantee, don't promise this to user

--- Technical Support ---

video not playing - common issues: 1) check internet 2) clear browser cache 3) try different
browser (chrome recommended) 4) disable adblock/vpn temporarily 5) app users - update to
latest version

cant login even with correct password - could be account locked after 5 failed attempts,
locked for 30 min automatically, or could be browser cookie issue, tell them try incognito

quiz not submitting - usually a connectivity blip, tell user to check answers saved
(auto-save every 30 sec), refresh page, answers should still be there, if lost then contact
support with screenshot

app crashing on android/ios - ask app version + phone OS version, tell to reinstall, if
persists escalate to tech team with device logs

download for offline viewing not working - offline mode only available on Pro/Premium plans,
also needs at least 500mb free storage, videos expire after 30 days offline (need to
reconnect to renew)

--- Subscription / Pricing ---

pricing tiers we have:
Basic = free, limited courses, ads, no certificate
Pro = 499/month or 4999/year (2 months free basically), full catalog, certificates, no ads
Premium = 999/month or 9999/year, everything in pro + 1-on-1 mentor sessions (2/month) +
offline download + priority support

student discount - yes 20% off Pro/Premium with valid student ID verification, verify via
upload of student ID card, takes 24-48hr to verify

can I switch from monthly to annual - yes anytime from Settings>Subscription>Upgrade,
prorated credit applied automatically

team/institution pricing - separate, need to contact sales team directly, not self-serve,
minimum 10 seats

is there a family plan - not currently, on roadmap, no ETA to give users

--- Escalation triggers noted by agents ---

if user says "this is ridiculous" or "worst platform" or similar frustrated language ->
de-escalate, apologize, offer to connect human agent
if user asks something totally outside our scope like general coding help unrelated to our
courses, or asks about a different company entirely -> politely say out of scope, redirect
if same question asked 3+ times rephrased -> bot probably not understanding, escalate
proactively
